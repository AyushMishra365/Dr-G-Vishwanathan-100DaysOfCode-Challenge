## Problem 1: Maximum Product of Three Numbers

**Approach:**
- Sorted the array.
- Compared the product of the three largest numbers with the product of the two smallest (possibly negative) numbers and the largest number.
- Returned the maximum of the two values.

**Time Complexity:** O(n log n)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)

**Performance**
- Runtime: **15 ms** (Beats **80.39%**)
- Memory: **48.14 MB** (Beats **41.98%**)

## Problem 2: Three Pairwise Maximums

**Approach:**
- Sorted the three given values.
- Checked whether the two largest values were equal.
- If they were equal, constructed a valid triplet using the smallest value twice and the largest value once.
- Otherwise, printed that no valid solution exists.

**Time Complexity:** O(1)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)
