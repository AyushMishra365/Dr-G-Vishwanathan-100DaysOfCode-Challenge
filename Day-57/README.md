## Problem 1: Assign Cookies

**Approach:**
- Sorted both the greed factor array and the cookie sizes.
- Used two pointers to greedily assign the smallest possible cookie that satisfies each child.
- Counted the maximum number of content children.

**Time Complexity:** O(n log n + m log m)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)

**Performance**
- Runtime: **14 ms** (Beats **92.15%**)
- Memory: **51.33 MB** (Beats **74.51%**)

---

## Day 57 Takeaways

- Practiced the greedy strategy of sorting before making assignments.
- Learned how two pointers efficiently maximize valid matches.
- Reinforced that assigning the smallest sufficient resource preserves larger resources for later.
