### Problems Overview

#### 405A. Gravity Flip

A simulation problem involving columns of cubes affected by gravity.

**My Approach**

- Stored the heights of all columns in an array.
- Sorted the array in non-decreasing order.
- Printed the resulting arrangement after gravity had taken effect.

**What I Learned**

The key observation was that after gravity acts on the cubes, the columns naturally end up arranged in sorted order.

**Complexity**

- Time Complexity: O(n log n)
- Space Complexity: O(1)
