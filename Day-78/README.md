## Problems Solved:

### 1. 205. Isomorphic Strings

**Problem:**  
Determine whether two strings are isomorphic, meaning characters in one string can be replaced to obtain the other while preserving their order and ensuring that no two characters map to the same character.

**My Approach:**  
Used two frequency/index arrays to maintain the mapping between characters of both strings. For every position, I check whether the existing mappings are consistent. If a mismatch is found, the strings are not isomorphic.

**Complexity:**  
- Time Complexity: O(n)
- Space Complexity: O(1)
