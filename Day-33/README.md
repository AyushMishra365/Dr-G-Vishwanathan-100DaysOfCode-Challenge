### Problem Overview

#### 70. Climbing Stairs

Used an iterative dynamic programming approach with two variables to generate Fibonacci numbers, eliminating the need for recursion or extra memory.

#### 8. String to Integer (atoi)

Implemented the parsing process step by step by skipping whitespaces, handling optional signs, detecting overflow, and constructing the integer digit by digit.

#### A. Elephant

Applied a greedy strategy by taking the largest possible 5-step moves until the destination was reached, minimizing the total number of moves.

#### B. Drinks

Computed the total percentage of orange juice across all drinks and divided it by the number of drinks to obtain the final concentration.
