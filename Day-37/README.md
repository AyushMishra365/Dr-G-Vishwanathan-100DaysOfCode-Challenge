### Problem Overview

#### 507. Perfect Number

Calculated the sum of all proper divisors by iterating only up to the square root of the number and adding divisor pairs whenever a divisor was found. Finally compared the sum with the original number.

#### 3754. Sum and Multiply

Constructed the required number by ignoring zeros while simultaneously maintaining the digit sum. The final answer was obtained by multiplying the accumulated number with its digit sum.

#### Codeforces 4A – Watermelon

Used the simple mathematical observation that a watermelon can be divided into two even positive parts only when its weight is even and greater than two.

#### Codeforces 459B – Pashmak and Flowers

Sorted the array to obtain the minimum and maximum beauty values. Counted their occurrences to determine the number of valid pairs producing the maximum beauty difference.
